# Mathematical scope of the collective diagnostic

Fix topology, features, query and trained binary-sign predictor p(S) in [0,1]. Let Q be ONE specified
joint distribution of all signs. Let F_r be the sigma-field generated by signs at distance <=r, nested
with increasing r. For two conditionally independent completions S1,S2 distributed as Q(.|F_r), define

    C_Q(r) = E[(p(S1)-p(S2))^2]/2 = E Var_Q(p(S)|F_r).

The second equality expands the square conditional on F_r, using conditional independence. The
expectation covers local configurations as well as completions. Zero is equivalent to p being F_r-
measurable Q-almost surely: E[(p-E[p|F_r])^2]=0. For r<=s, conditional total variance gives

    C_Q(r) = C_Q(s) + E Var(E[p|F_s] | F_r) >= C_Q(s).

These are standard probability identities, not proposed novelty. The diagnostic depends on the binary
prediction function and chosen Q, not its continuous relaxation. On a particular observed configuration,
Var(p | S_near=s_near) need not decrease: for two independent fair bits and p=AND, unconditional variance
is 3/16, whereas conditioning on the first bit being one gives 1/4 (the population conditional average
is 1/8). The real-network stress curves fix observed local signs and change their completion laws;
no monotonicity certificate is claimed for them.

For N independent pairs, Z_i=(p_i1-p_i2)^2/2 in [0,1/2]. Hoeffding gives

    P(|mean Z - mean E Z| >= epsilon) <= 2 exp(-8 N epsilon^2).

Thus epsilon=(1/2)*sqrt(log(2/delta)/(2N)). This also applies to independent, non-identically distributed
pairs across FIXED queries; it bounds MC error in their mean conditional variance. Clip intervals to
[0,1/4]. For m pre-specified means use delta=alpha/m for simultaneous coverage. Dependence between cells
does not invalidate this union bound. It does not address Q misspecification or finite query/seed coverage.

# Controlled functions and required distance

Consensus and redundancy have k disjoint target-to-source paths of length d+1, with positive connectors.
Only terminal signs X_j at edge distance d encode label Y; a separate local sign Z is independent.
Features contain unsigned source markers and independent noise only. Y=sign(sum X_j), k odd, under
independent uniform X_j for consensus; X_j=Y for redundancy with fair shared Y. For every r<d, observable
signs consist of constants and possibly Z, independently of fair Y; topology, degrees and features
also carry no label information. No predictor of those inputs exceeds chance in population. At r=d,
Y is determined. Under redundancy, independent flips create off-support configurations; true-Q completion
changes the shared latent Y jointly. Both diagnostics are useful only when their distributions are named.

The binary solver is ell=2Y+0.5Z, p=sigmoid(ell), correctly classifying with margin >=1.5. Majority's unique
multilinear extension is the expectation of majority when each bit is independently positive with
probability (1+s_j)/2. At a binary vertex its derivative equals half its signed finite difference; it is
zero for every terminal bit if |sum X_j|>1. In redundant configurations k>=3, every single terminal flip
leaves ell unchanged. The local nuisance still has derivative 0.5 and finite effect 1, so both individual
reach and mass radii are zero despite nonzero distant dependence. This is a function-level counterexample,
not a claim that relative individual sensitivity itself is incorrectly defined.

For r>=0 and r<d, conditional on Z=z, the exact true-Q variance is

    [sigmoid(2+0.5z)-sigmoid(-2+0.5z)]^2 / 4;

for r>=d it is zero. Sparse chain Y is the product of independent path signs. Missing its farthest sign
keeps Y fair; ordinary product gradients and all single flips detect the distant chain. Composing signs
with h(s)=(3s-s^3)/2 leaves binary predictions identical but makes chain derivatives zero at +/-1. This
separates collective dependence, single-flip dependence and local relaxation derivatives.

A degenerate Q can give C_Q=0 while its fixed prediction differs from the original prediction. Pair variance
must therefore accompany original-prediction fidelity; it is not a standalone original-neighbourhood
sufficiency certificate. The practical budget study must test cropping/step removal themselves.
