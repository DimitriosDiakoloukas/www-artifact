## Scientific limits

The learned known-distance failure is synthetic and threshold/domain-dependent. Native stress laws are
not natural conditionals, and loss/decoder/confidence confound architecture-only attribution. Count matching
does not match edit locations. Crop locality and global layer placement are different tests. Performance
criteria use 128-query cohorts with few negative labels; full-test schedule evaluation, native matched
location constraints, future-snapshot fidelity and unchanged-snapshot embedding-cache savings are untested.
